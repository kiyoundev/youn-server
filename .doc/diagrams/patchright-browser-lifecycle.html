Patchright Browser Lifecycle

Two independent triggers — a 6h scheduled poll and ad-hoc adds — each lazily launch and close the shared Patchright browser on their own terms, coordinating only through its shared state, never with each other directly.

recycle_browser() and its daily scheduler job are removed in #47 — nothing runs long enough between a batch draining and the next one starting to need a periodic forced refresh anymore.
